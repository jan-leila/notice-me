Notice Me

Your watch shows the few tasks at the top of your list, times your focus on the one you start, and nudges you when an active task turns into sitting.

Task list

Tasks come from a project on your Vikunja server, highest priority first, then oldest first. Starting one on the watch moves it to the “in progress” column of the project's kanban board; finishing it marks it done.

Focus timer

A started task runs rounds of focus and breaks. The watch buzzes at every change, also when the app is closed.

Movement

While you focus on a task with the active label, the watch notices sitting still and buzzes you back to the task.
